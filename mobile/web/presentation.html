Part 1 • Problem & Target Users

The Dual Dilemma: Reactive Alarms & The Indian Diet Blindspot

👥 Target Users

  • 850,000+ Type 1 Diabetics living in India, with rapid pediatric diagnoses every year.
  • Patients take exogenous insulin daily; dosing is an unforgiving tightrope walk.
  • Severe Hypo (<54 mg/dL) causes 30% of emergency room hospitalizations.
  • Constant nocturnal anxiety: Fear of seizures and unconsciousness while sleeping.

⚠️ Problem 1: Reactive Alarms

  • Current CGMs (Dexcom/Libre) alarm AFTER dropping below 70 mg/dL.
  • At <70 mg/dL, patients are already dizzy, trembling, and impaired.
  • Zero early-warning window to digest food before crashing.
  • Leads to panic binge-eating and dangerous rebound spikes to 300+ mg/dL.

🍛 Problem 2: Indian Diet Dilemma

  • Western apps (MyFitnessPal) demand raw gram weights: "120g boiled yellow dal".
  • Nobody weighs a chapati at an Indian family dinner or wedding!
  • Indian meals are composite and served in Rotis & Katoris.
  • Results in blind dosage guessing and frequent post-meal crashes.

Part 3 • Key Features & Safety

Clinical Features & Safety-First Decision Support

⚡ Velocity-Aware Glucose Gauge

  • Dynamic Rate of Change: Calculates glucose velocity in mg/dL per minute.
  • Instant Clinical Triage: Green In-Range (70-180), Amber Warning (54-70), Red Critical (<54).
  • 90% Confidence Interval Cone: Visualizes biological variability ($\pm 1.96 \times \text{RMSE}$) so patients understand prediction uncertainty.

⏱️ Clinical "Rule of 15" Protocol

  • ADA Gold Standard: Official protocol for treating hypoglycemia safely.
  • Step 1: Take exactly 15g fast carbs (fruit juice, 3 tsp sugar, jaggery).
  • Step 2: 15-minute countdown timer stops panic overeating.
  • Step 3: Phone buzzes at 15m to prompt mandatory blood glucose re-check.

📜 Non-Device CDS Regulatory Compliance

  • Strictly designed as Clinical Decision Support (CDS) under CDSCO and FDA guidelines.
  • Persistent regulatory disclaimers throughout all interfaces.
  • Does not autonomously command insulin pumps; informs and empowers the human patient.

🍽️ Dual-Mode Indian Food Logging

  • Camera AI Scan: Tableware-calibrated dish detection (9cm katori optical anchor).
  • Manual Household Builder: One-tap buttons for [-] 2 Rotis [+] and [-] 1 Katori Dal [+].
